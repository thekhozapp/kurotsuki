The name

A black moon is the new moon. You can't see it, but it's there, and every cycle starts with it. That's how good technology should feel: invisible until you need it, and then exactly right.

What we make

KHOZ, for everything around you.

KHOZ is one app for the neighbourhood: find people and local businesses, book a salon or a turf, ask the locals, run your society, plan a housewarming, split the bills. And for the people who run those businesses, a toolkit that fits in a pocket.

Built in India, for how India actually lives. Money goes straight from your UPI app to the other person. KHOZ never holds it.

How we work

Three rules we don't bend.

01

Never hold the money

Payments go from your UPI app to theirs. We show the receipt, the reminder and the record, never a wallet in the middle.

02

Local first

Built for a lane in Kondapur before a city on a map: Indian languages, Indian festivals, Indian ways of paying and asking.

03

Quiet by design

Fewer screens, fewer notifications, the next step on the button. Software that gets out of the way of the people using it.
