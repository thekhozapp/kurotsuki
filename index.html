How we work

Three rules we don't bend.

01

Never hold the money

Payments go from your UPI app to theirs. We keep the receipt, the reminder and the record, never a wallet in the middle.

02

Local first

Built for a lane in Kondapur before a city on a map: Indian languages, festivals, and the ways people here pay and ask.

03

Quiet by design

Fewer screens, fewer pings, the next step on the button. Software that gets out of the way of the people using it.

Every cycle

From a dark idea to a full moon.

  1. NoticeNew moon

    It starts in the dark: something in a neighbourhood that's harder than it should be.

  2. ListenCrescent

    We sit with the salon owner, the tutor, the society secretary, and hear how they really work.

  3. SketchFirst quarter

    Half an idea on paper, shown to the people it's for before a line of code.

  4. BuildGibbous

    Small, tested, in their language, for the phone already in their pocket.

  5. ShipFull moon

    It reaches everyone at once, and we watch it land.

  6. WatchWaning

    Real use tells the truth. We read every message sent to support.

  7. FixLast quarter

    What's rough gets smoothed. What nobody uses gets taken away.

  8. AgainOld moon

    Then back to the dark, to notice the next thing.
